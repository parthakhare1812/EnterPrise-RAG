4. CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

The customer satisfaction score for Q3 was 4.1 out of 5, compared with 3.9 in Q2. The repeat purchase rate was 28%. The average first-response time for customer support was 3 hours and 20 minutes.

The three most frequently reported customer issues were:
1. Delivery arriving later than the promised date.
2. Product availability information not matching actual inventory.
3. Delayed refunds after a returned item had been received.

Customer feedback indicated that delivery updates were useful when accurate. Customers were less satisfied when estimated delivery dates changed without an explanation.

5. INVENTORY AND FULFILMENT

Inventory record accuracy was estimated at 93%. The target is 98%. The company recorded 146 stockout incidents during the quarter. Electronics and Fashion accounted for 62% of those incidents.

The average warehouse order processing time was 7.5 hours. The operational target is 6 hours. The South region experienced the highest delivery delay rate, with 12% of orders arriving later than the promised date.

The operations team proposed a weekly cycle-count process for high-value products and a daily exception report for stock discrepancies.

6. KEY RISKS

Risk A — Inventory mismatch
Incorrect stock records can lead to cancelled orders and customer dissatisfaction. The proposed mitigation is more frequent cycle counting and reconciliation between store and warehouse records.

Risk B — Delivery delays
Delivery delays are concentrated in selected South-region routes. The proposed mitigation is route-level performance monitoring and revised delivery estimates.

Risk C — High fashion returns
Fashion has an 8.4% return rate. The proposed mitigation is clearer product sizing information and improved product descriptions.

Risk D — Refund processing
Customers reported delays after returned goods were received. The proposed mitigation is a daily refund queue review and an escalation rule for cases older than five working days.

8. DEFINITIONS

Quarter-on-quarter growth: The percentage change in a metric compared with the immediately preceding quarter.

Average order value: Total order revenue divided by the number of orders.

On-time delivery rate: The percentage of delivered orders that arrived on or before the promised delivery date.

Inventory record accuracy: The percentage of checked inventory records that match the physically counted stock.

Repeat purchase rate: The percentage of customers who placed more than one order during the measurement period.
